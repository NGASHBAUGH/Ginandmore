Our Story

Gin&More was born from a passion for rugby and a love for high-quality spirits.

The idea came about during post-match gatherings with friends, where the desire to create a unique gin that reflected Italian tradition and the energy of the rugby world emerged.

After months of research and experimentation, Gin&More was launched: a premium gin made with carefully selected botanicals, including juniper berries from the Italian Alps and other natural ingredients that give it a distinctive and balanced flavor.

The name "Gin&More" represents not only the gin itself but also the lifestyle associated with it: friendship, sharing, and the joy of living moments together, just like on the rugby field.

Today, Gin&More is appreciated by gin enthusiasts and rugby lovers alike, offering a unique experience that combines Italian craftsmanship with the passion for sport.

Our Gins

We craft premium gins inspired by Italian traditions and the spirit of rugby.

Classic Dry Gin

A balanced London Dry style gin with prominent juniper, citrus notes, and subtle herbal undertones. Perfect for classic cocktails.

Rugby Gin

Our signature gin celebrating the rugby spirit – bold, energetic flavors with Italian botanicals and a hint of adventure.

Navy Strength Gin

Higher proof for intense flavor. Robust juniper and spice notes, ideal for strong cocktails or sipping.

Barrel Aged Gin

Aged in oak barrels for a smooth, rich character with vanilla, caramel, and woody notes complementing the botanicals.
